Scholarships to apply for (can make it nearly free)
  • E-Grantz (Kerala govt) — For SC/ST/OBC/OEC students. Covers tuition + monthly stipend. No income limit for SC/ST/OEC. Apply at egrantz.kerala.gov.in after admission. Or visit any Akshaya Centre.
  • Snehapoorvam Scholarship — For BPL family children. Apply through the college.
  • KPCR Scholarship — For economically backward forward communities (family income < ₹1 lakh/yr).
  • NSP (National Scholarship Portal) — Central govt scholarships. Apply at scholarships.gov.in. Open Aug–Nov each year.
  • Pragati Scholarship (AICTE) — For girl students in degree/diploma programs. Apply after admission.
Step 5 — exact next steps right now (June 2026)
  • 1
    Register at Kannur University CAP portal TODAY — Registration closes around June 12, 2026. Go to admission.kannuruniversity.ac.in → "Register Online." Fill name, DOB, category, email. Get application number.
  • 2
    Fill in college preferences (options) — List up to 20 college-course combinations in priority order. Put govt/aided colleges first. Trial allotment comes before actual allotment — use it to adjust options.
  • 3
    Also register on Calicut University CAP (admission.uoc.ac.in) — Same process. Last date June 10–12, 2026. Covers more college options in Kozhikode area.
  • 4
    Wait for trial allotment — This is NOT final. It gives you an idea of where she might get. You can still change options after trial allotment.
  • 5
    First allotment result — Usually late June. Pay the fee online + download allotment memo within the deadline (usually 3–5 days). Miss this = lose the seat.
  • 6
    Report to college — Carry allotment memo + all documents. Second and third allotments happen after — she can upgrade to a better college/course if she stays in the pool.
  • 7
    Apply for E-Grantz — After admission is confirmed. Go to egrantz.kerala.gov.in or nearest Akshaya Centre. Covers tuition fee if eligible.
  • 8
    Apply for NSP & other scholarships — From August onward once the academic year begins. Keep income certificate, caste certificate, and Aadhaar ready.
