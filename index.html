BCA — AI + Cloud + DevOps explained simply
What each part actually means.
AI — Artificial Intelligence
Teaching computers to think
Like the tech behind ChatGPT, Netflix recommendations, Google Search. You build programs that see, understand, and decide on their own.
Cloud Computing
The internet's power station
AWS (Amazon), Azure (Microsoft), Google Cloud. Every app on your phone runs on cloud. You manage and build this invisible infrastructure.
DevOps
The speed engineer
Automates how software is built, tested and released. Uses Docker, Kubernetes, GitHub. One of the highest paid IT roles in India right now.

What to do now
Step-by-step action plan.
Today is June 6, 2026. Kerala CAP closes June 12 — 6 days left. Register on both university portals right now. It takes 20 minutes and costs ₹500.
  • 1
    Register on Kannur University CAP — TODAY
    Go to the website below → Click "Register" → Enter Plus 2 marks, personal details, email, phone → Pay ₹500 online → Get application number. Takes 20 minutes. Closes June 12.
    admission.kannuruniversity.ac.in
  • 2
    Register on Calicut University CAP — TODAY / TOMORROW
    Same process. Covers colleges in Kozhikode including St. Joseph's Devagiri. Doubles her college options. Closes June 10–12.
    admission.uoc.ac.in
  • 3
    Fill your college preference list carefully
    Put the best college you want FIRST. Fill all available slots (up to 20). Do not leave slots empty. The system gives the best available college based on marks + list order. Suggested order: Brennen College → CAS Kannur → Nirmalagiri → Sir Syed → Taliparamba Arts & Science → Chinmaya Institute (if choosing BCA).
  • 4
    If interested in Mangalore — apply directly to Srinivas or Yenepoya
    No CAP system in Mangalore. Apply directly on the college website. Fill online form → pay ₹30,000 token fee (adjusted in Year 1 tuition) → merit-based direct admission. No entrance exam needed.
    srinivasuniversity.edu.in yiascm.yenepoya.edu.in
  • 5
    Wait for Kerala CAP trial allotment (mid-June)
    Trial allotment is not final — it just shows where she might get. She can still change preferences after seeing trial allotment. Final allotment comes after.
  • 6
    Pay fee within deadline when final allotment arrives (late June)
    When final allotment comes, pay the fee online within 3–5 days and download the allotment memo. Miss this window = lose the seat permanently. Do not delay.
  • 7
    Report to college with all documents (July 2026)
    Carry original documents + allotment memo + passport photos. Classes typically start first week of July. She can still participate in 2nd and 3rd allotment rounds for a better college if she stays in the pool.
  • 8
    Apply for E-Grantz scholarship after admission — very important
    Visit the website or nearest Akshaya Centre within 1 month of joining. For SC/ST/OBC/OEC students — covers full tuition + monthly stipend. Apply for NSP on scholarships.gov.in from August onwards.
    egrantz.kerala.gov.in scholarships.gov.in
  • 9
    From Day 1 of college — start free AWS course online
    Register for free at aws.amazon.com/training. Start "AWS Cloud Practitioner Essentials" course (free). Appear for the exam in Year 2. This one certification adds ₹1–2 LPA to her starting salary regardless of which college she joins.
    aws.amazon.com/training

Make it almost free
Scholarships she must apply for.
  • E-Grantz — Kerala Government
    For SC / ST / OBC / OEC students in Kerala colleges. Full tuition fee refunded + monthly stipend. No income limit for SC/ST. Apply within 1 month of joining college.
    egrantz.kerala.gov.in · Or any Akshaya Centre
  • NSP — National Scholarship Portal
    Central government scholarships for students with family income below ₹2.5L/year. Opens August every year. ₹10,000–₹25,000/year. Apply after joining college.
    scholarships.gov.in
  • Pragati Scholarship — AICTE (for girl students)
    For girl students in degree/diploma programmes. ₹50,000/year covering tuition and incidentals. She qualifies directly being a girl student.
    aicte-pragati-saksham-gov.in
  • ASAP Kerala — Additional Skill Acquisition Programme
    Free skill training programme inside Kerala colleges. Spoken English, IT skills, soft skills. Automatically enrolled after joining. Certificate adds value to resume.
    Through your college — fully free
  • KPCR Scholarship — Kerala
    For forward community students with family income below ₹1 lakh/year. Covers tuition fee. Apply through the college office after admission.
    Apply through college
  • SSP Karnataka — for Mangalore colleges
    For SC/ST/OBC students in Karnataka colleges. Full tuition fee reimbursement. If she studies in Mangalore and is eligible, apply here.
    ssp.postmatric.karnataka.gov.in
