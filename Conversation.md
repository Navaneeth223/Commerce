Step 1: Start the Conversation

When you enter the admission office:

Simple English:

Good morning.

I am interested in joining BCA. I would like to know about the course, fees, placements, hostel facilities, and admission process.

That sentence alone starts the entire discussion.

Step 2: Important Questions to Ask

Keep your phone notes open and write answers.

A. Course Details

Ask:

Which BCA specialization do you offer?

Examples:

BCA
BCA AI & ML
BCA Data Science
BCA Cloud Computing
BCA Cyber Security

Ask:

Which specialization has better placements?

Ask:

Can I see the syllabus?

Ask:

Are industry certifications included?

Examples:

AWS
Azure
Google Cloud
Python
Data Analytics
AI
B. Placement Questions (Very Important)

Many colleges advertise placements.

Ask:

What was the highest package for BCA students last year?

Ask:

What was the average package?

Ask:

How many students got placed?

Ask:

Which companies visited the campus?

Write down company names.

Ask:

Can I see placement records for the last two years?

This is one of the strongest questions you can ask.

C. Internship Questions

Ask:

Do you provide internships during the course?

Ask:

Are internships mandatory?

Ask:

Are internships paid or unpaid?

D. Fees Questions

Write everything carefully.

Ask:

What is the complete fee structure?

Ask:

Are there any additional charges?

Examples:

Exam fees
Lab fees
Hostel fees
Transport fees
Uniform fees

Ask:

Can fees be paid semester-wise?

E. Scholarship Questions

Ask:

Do you offer scholarships?

Ask:

Is there any scholarship based on Plus Two marks?

Ask:

Are there scholarships for Kerala students?

F. Hostel Questions

Ask:

What are the hostel fees?

Ask:

Is food included?

Ask:

How many students stay in a room?

Ask:

What is the hostel timing?

Ask:

Can I visit the hostel?

Try to physically see the hostel.

G. Teaching Quality Questions

Ask:

How many faculty members are there in the BCA department?

Ask:

Do faculty members have industry experience?

Ask:

Are there practical projects every semester?

H. Future Opportunities

Ask:

After completing BCA, what higher studies do students usually pursue?

Examples:

MCA
MSc
MBA

Ask:

Do you provide guidance for higher studies?

Step 3: Questions Specifically for AI-Focused BCA

Since you're interested in AI and Full Stack Development, ask:

Is AI taught practically or only theoretically?

Ask:

Do students build real projects?

Ask:

Are there AI labs available?

Ask:

Do students work with Python, Machine Learning, and Cloud Computing?

Step 4: Important Documents to Carry

Carry originals and copies if possible.

Bring:
10th Mark Sheet
12th Mark Sheet
Aadhaar Card
Passport Size Photos
Transfer Certificate (if available)
Migration Certificate (if available)
Category Certificate (if applicable)
Income Certificate (for scholarship purposes)

Also carry a pen and notebook.

Step 5: What They Might Say

Sometimes counselors say:

We have 100% placement.

Your response:

Could you please show placement records or statistics?

If they say:

Many companies visit our campus.

Your response:

Which companies visited for BCA students specifically?

If they say:

Our students get very good packages.

Your response:

What was the average package last year?

Step 7: If You Feel Nervous

Use this sentence:

Sorry, English is not my first language. Could you please explain slowly?

People in admission offices hear this often and will usually help.

A Ready-to-Use 1-Minute Introduction

Practice this before you go:

Hello Sir/Madam. My name is Navaneeth. I am from Kerala. I am interested in joining the BCA program. I would like to know about the course structure, specializations, placements, internships, hostel facilities, fees, and scholarship opportunities. I am also interested in AI and Full Stack Development, so I would like to understand which specialization would be best for my career goals.

If you use this introduction and the question list above, you'll sound organized and serious, and you'll get much more useful information from the meeting.
